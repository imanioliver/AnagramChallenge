About Rhythm & Range

Movement is the foundation for how you live, feel, and function.

Rhythm & Range helps people build a stronger, more connected relationship with their bodies through intentional movement. Whether your goal is to improve mobility, get stronger, move with more ease, or sharpen your athletic performance, we take a balanced approach built to support your body for the long run.

Movement should feel purposeful and empowering, never rushed, forced, or one-dimensional. Our services include:

  • Mobility and flexibility sessions
  • Mindful movement and recovery practices
  • Functional strength and conditioning
  • Speed, agility, and performance training
  • Group classes and guided sessions
  • Workshops, wellness events, and community experiences
  • Digital movement resources and programs

Every offering is designed to build a body that is not only strong, but also adaptable, resilient, and well supported.

Your Coach

Meet Jenea McCammon

Jenea McCammon is a wellness professional and performance specialist with a background in athletics, strength training, and functional movement. A former professional track athlete for Guyana and the current Physical Fitness Coordinator for a large law enforcement agency, she has worked with hundreds of people at every fitness level, including tactical teams, community members, and young athletes.

Jenea’s approach blends mobility, restorative movement, and practical body mechanics to reduce aches, improve joint function, and build resilience against stress. She is passionate about helping people keep their strength and freedom of movement as they age, and she believes longevity is built through consistency, awareness, and intentional care of the body.

Her work focuses on sustainable movement practices that support both physical performance and nervous system regulation.

  • Master’s in Sport Science & Fitness Management
  • NASM Certified Personal Trainer
  • Former Professional Track Athlete
  • Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT 200)
